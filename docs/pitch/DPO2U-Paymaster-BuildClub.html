The barrier to adoption

The seal costs DUST.
That's a wall in front of every dApp.

  • DUST is non-transferable — only NIGHT holders generate it.
  • To prove compliance, a dApp must hold NIGHT and babysit DUST regeneration.
  • So most don't — compliance stays theater, and adoption stalls.
  • Meanwhile the ecosystem's NIGHT generates DUST that sits idle.
Speaker
Reframe the DUST constraint as an ADOPTION barrier, not a business problem. The wall is why the seal hasn't spread.

The core reframe. Protocols are won by adoption. We remove the DUST wall entirely by sponsoring compliance for free, funded by pooled idle DUST — not by invoicing users.
Dust Comply

Pooled DUST that confers compliance.

  • Participants delegate idle DUST — and keep custody of their NIGHT.
  • The pool seals compliance for dApps and confers the seal to the participants themselves.
  • DPO2U becomes the manager of the ecosystem's accumulated DUST and its compliance layer.
  • The prize is the protocol position — not a per-transaction fee.
Speaker
Name the concept: Dust Comply. Holders don't get paid cash — they get compliance status and a healthier ecosystem they already hold NIGHT in. DPO2U's moat is being the DUST-management + compliance hub.
How it works

Three legs, one loop.

1
Delegate
registerNightUtxosForDustGeneration(…, poolDustAddress)
Holder cedes idle DUST — keeps NIGHT. → PaymentGateway.registerDustDelegation
2
Seal — free
balanceUnboundTransaction(tx, poolKeys, { tokenKindsToBalance: ['dust'] })
Pool pays the DUST fee to seal a dApp's attestation. → ComplianceRegistry.recordSponsoredAttestation
3
Confer & monetize
dApps: sealed free. Big players: pay to tap the pooled DUST for their own tx — compliance is the gate. → recordSponsoredAttestation + FeeDistributor.recordLpRevenue
Speaker
Every arrow is an on-chain circuit we shipped this sprint. Leg 3 is conferral, not billing — the ledger records contribution + compliance, no money.
Why it must be on-chain

Rip out Midnight and the seal breaks.

  • DUST-generation redirect is a Midnight ledger primitive — nothing else has it.
  • Non-custodial delegation: holder keeps custody, only the fee resource flows.
  • Score-private seal: prove score ≥ threshold without revealing the score.
  • It makes the seal free and self-sustaining across the ecosystem.
Speaker
Blockchain-necessity test in the seal's language. Midnight is the native half of DPO2U's dual-chain design (Stellar seals the public proof; Midnight fuels the free, self-funding seal).
